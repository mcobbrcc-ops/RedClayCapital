# 90-day organic content and measurement backlog

Start after the verified release. Priority reflects user intent and content gaps, not measured rankings or demand. Use qualified inquiries and useful seller conversations as outcomes; do not treat call/text taps as completed conversations.

| Window | Priority and work | Dependency / completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–7 | Capture a real 90-day Search Console and analytics baseline by landing page, query, state, and device. Check indexing and redirects for all preserved routes. | Authorized account access; export dated reports and annotate the release. Separate unknown attribution from organic. |
| Days 1–14 | Review the first accepted inquiries from NC, GA, OH, and `/get-offer` for source preservation and contact quality. | Owner-only RCI access; exclude synthetic test records; use accepted and qualified counts without exposing seller details. |

| Days 75–90 | Choose the next two content updates from real queries and qualified lead themes; retire low-value duplication only with a preservation plan. | Written decision log, source checks, and exact URL mapping. |

## Two future experiments

1. Hero emphasis: compare clarity about an as-is review with clarity about less preparation. Primary metric: durable accepted inquiries per eligible landing session. Guardrail: the proportion that become a qualified seller conversation, plus mistaken expectations about guaranteed offers. Run only after reliable consent-aware measurement exists; decide sample size and stopping criteria in advance.
2. Optional detail placement: compare collapsed optional fields with a short second step. Primary metric: completed durable inquiries per form start. Guardrail: usable contact rate, downstream qualification, duplicate rate, and mobile error rate. Do not declare a winner from clicks or a handful of submissions.

Do not buy advertising or introduce paid analytics/content services as part of this backlog. Quarterly source checks are a reasonable starting cadence; update sooner if a cited official resource changes or a seller question reveals ambiguity.
